What is NetPlay?

A networking class built as a world you can explore.

NetPlay combines classroom learning with 2D campus exploration. Students move between rooms, follow objectives, study lessons, answer quizzes, complete laboratory work, and build a visible academic record inside one connected game.

Learning does not stop at reading. Every concept becomes an action.

The player studies a topic, proves understanding through a quiz, then applies it in a hands-on networking activity. Progress, energy, coins, and level objectives give every step a clear purpose.

The learning flow
  1. 01Explore the campus and follow the current objective.
  2. 02Study the lesson and review supporting resources.
  3. 03Pass the quiz and complete the laboratory activity.
  4. 04Track progress until the certificate is unlocked.
